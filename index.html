Key Thesis
Hospitality is evolving from a commodity asset class to a PLACE-MAKING PLATFORM. The most interesting opportunities emerge at the intersection: developers who operate, operators who develop, and capital that partners strategically. Mixed-use bundling (hotels + residences + retail + public realm) creates durable competitive advantage and defends against commoditization.
